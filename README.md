EMOM · KETTLEBELL + PUSH-UPS — how to host it & add to your iPhone
==================================================================

This whole app is ONE file: index.html (the icon is baked in).
Everything saves on your phone (localStorage) — your reps, notes, checkmarks.


=====================================================================
OPTION A — GitHub Pages (what you asked for)
=====================================================================
1. Go to https://github.com  and create a new repository
   (name it e.g. "emom" — make it Public).
2. On the new repo page click "uploading an existing file" and drop in
   index.html  (this file's folder). Commit.
3. Repo → Settings → Pages.
   - Source: "Deploy from a branch"
   - Branch: main  /  (root)  → Save.
4. Wait ~1 minute, refresh. It shows a link like:
      https://YOURNAME.github.io/emom/
5. Open that link on your iPhone in SAFARI.
6. Share button (square + arrow) → "Add to Home Screen" → Add.
7. Tap the orange kettlebell icon and train.

(Android: open the link in Chrome → ⋮ → "Add to Home screen" / "Install app".)


=====================================================================
OPTION B — even faster, no GitHub (drag & drop)
=====================================================================
1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Drag the "emom-kettlebell" folder onto the page (no signup needed).
3. It gives you a link → open on your iPhone in Safari → Add to Home Screen.


=====================================================================
QUICK LOOK ON YOUR MAC (optional)
=====================================================================
Just double-click index.html — it opens in your browser and works fully.


=====================================================================
HOW TO USE IT
=====================================================================
• Pick a WEEK (tabs at the top), then a DAY card. Tap it to expand.
• You'll see the warm-up, the minute-by-minute EMOM plan, and the cooldown.
• Tap "START EMOM":
   - 5-second "Get ready", then the clock runs.
   - Each minute: do the target reps for that move, rest the remaining seconds.
   - It BEEPS + buzzes when a new minute starts, shows the current move,
     target reps, what's next, and minutes remaining. Screen stays awake.
   - Controls: reset ↺ · play/pause ▶ · skip ⏭.
• After (or instead of) the timer, type your ACTUAL reps per minute into the
   boxes, and jot a note. Tap the circle to mark the day complete.

PROGRESS TAB (bottom of the screen: Program | Progress)
• Builds itself from your logged reps and completed days — nothing extra to do.
• Shows: sessions done, total reps, minutes trained, current day-streak,
   program completion, a sessions-per-week chart, your total volume per
   movement (with your best single-minute set), and a dated session history.

THE PLAN
• 8 weeks, 5 day-cards each (train 4–5, rest when you need it).
• Reps start easy and climb each week. Deadlifts & rows join in Week 5.
• Days rotate kettlebell moves (swing, goblet, clean, press, row, deadlift)
   with the 4 push-up board grips: 🟥 chest · 🟨 triceps · 🟦 shoulders · 🟩 lats.
• Day 4 is "blocks" style (same move several minutes in a row); the rest rotate.
